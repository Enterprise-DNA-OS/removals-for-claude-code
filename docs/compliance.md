# The rules this system checks

`/compliance` (`node scripts/removals.mjs compliance`) checks the records against each rule below, and the CLI refuses the actions that would break one unless the operator gives a reason with `--override`, which goes in the audit log. Every period and limit is a setting on the company profile, so it matches your own terms, your state and New Zealand: `node scripts/removals.mjs set company_profile '{"storage_sale_notice_days":42}'`.

This is not legal advice. Removalists' terms, state consumer guidance and uncollected goods laws differ. Check each setting against your own contract of carriage and storage terms, and take advice before selling or disposing of anyone's goods.

| Rule | What the check looks for | Setting | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| WRITTEN-QUOTE | A booked move with no written quote sent before the customer accepted. `accept` refuses one. | `quote_valid_days` | NT Consumer Affairs, *Removalists*: get the quote in writing, with the full cost of freighting the goods, pick-up and delivery dates and any storage costs. [consumeraffairs.nt.gov.au](https://consumeraffairs.nt.gov.au/_resources/documents/for-consumers/shopping-for-goods-and-services/removalists.pdf) |
| STORAGE-COSTS | A quote for goods going into storage with no storage rate. | | Same source: the written quote includes any costs of storage. |
| VARIATION-IN-WRITING | A change to the price (more goods, more hours) agreed verbally only. `invoice` refuses until it is confirmed. | | Same source: if there are more goods at pick-up than quoted, the quote is adjusted, in writing. |
| INVENTORY-QUOTE | A quote with no inventory, or a move over the survey threshold quoted with no survey. | `survey_over_m3` (default 30 m3, your own rule) | NSW Government, *Choosing a removalist*: always ask for a written quote. [nsw.gov.au](https://www.nsw.gov.au/legal-and-justice/consumer-rights-and-protection/be-a-smart-consumer/choosing-a-removalist) |
| TRANSIT-INSURANCE | A booked move with no record of transit insurance offered and the answer. | | NSW Government, same page: a removalist can provide transit insurance covering goods while packed, moved and stored. |
| SUBCONTRACT-CONSENT | A move handed to another carrier (linehaul, another removalist) with no written consent from the customer. | | NT Consumer Affairs, same source: goods are often offloaded to another carrier without the customer's knowledge or approval. |
| HIGH-VALUE-DECLARED | An item at or over the high-value limit on an open job with no written declaration. | `high_value_limit` (default 1000) | Your own terms. Many removalists' conditions require written notice of items over a set value; the default follows one large Australian mover's published conditions. Set it to your own. |
| FATIGUE-STANDARD-HOURS | A driver of a heavy vehicle over the fatigue threshold rostered more than the daily work limit. `allocate` refuses one. | `max_work_hours` (12), `fatigue_gvm_t` (12) | NHVR, *Work and rest*: under standard hours a solo driver of a fatigue-regulated heavy vehicle (over 12 t GVM) works at most 12 hours in any 24, with 7 continuous hours of stationary rest. [nhvr.gov.au](https://www.nhvr.gov.au/work-and-rest) The check covers the 24 hour limit on rostered hours only; the shorter rest breaks and the 7 and 14 day limits are recorded in the work diary, not here. |
| LICENCE-CURRENT | A driver rostered to drive on a day after their licence expires. `allocate` refuses one. | `licence_warn_days` (30) | Your own rule. |
| STORAGE-RATE-NOTICE | A storage rate rise with less written notice than your storage terms give. `raise-rate` refuses one. | `storage_rate_notice_days` (default 28, a common figure in published storage conditions) | Your storage terms. |
| UNCOLLECTED-GOODS | Goods in storage sold or disposed of with no final notice, or sooner after it than the notice period. `dispose` refuses one. | `storage_notice_days` (30), `storage_final_days` (60), `storage_sale_notice_days` (28) | Your storage terms and the uncollected goods law that applies. In NSW: *Uncollected Goods Act 1995* (NSW), which sets procedures by the value of the goods. [legislation.nsw.gov.au](https://legislation.nsw.gov.au/view/html/inforce/current/act-1995-068) Other states have their own Acts. The defaults are a starting point only. |
| CARRIAGE-TERMS (NZ) | A booked move with no record of the carriage terms agreed (limited carriage, declared value, owner's risk or agreed terms). | | *Contract and Commercial Law Act 2017* (NZ), Part 5, carriage of goods. [legislation.govt.nz](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/2017/0005/latest/whole.html) |
| UNCOLLECTED-GOODS-NZ | As UNCOLLECTED-GOODS, for New Zealand. | as above | Your storage terms and Part 5 of the same Act. |
| CLAIM-RESPONSE | A damage claim with no reply after the response period. | `claim_response_days` (14, your own rule) | Your own rule. |

The overdue ladder for storage (`/storage-arrears`): late from the due date, an overdue notice from `storage_notice_days`, a final notice from `storage_final_days` once the overdue notice has gone, and sale or disposal no sooner than `storage_sale_notice_days` after the final notice.
